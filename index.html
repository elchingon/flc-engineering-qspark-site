Program overview

Built by FLC Engineering to make quantum approachable.

Fort Lewis College is a partner in Elevate Quantum, the U.S. Department of Commerce-designated Quantum Tech Hub for the Mountain West. Through Q-SPARK, FLC expands access to quantum education with workshops, mobile computer lab outreach, faculty-developed curriculum, and applied research opportunities.

The program creates early pathways into quantum computing, engineering, and advanced STEM fields for students across Southwest Colorado and the Four Corners region.

01

Quantum workshops

Introductory and advanced sessions for high school and college students using visuals, demos, software, and games.

02

Mobile lab outreach

Computer-lab-based outreach designed to reach rural and regional schools across Southwest Colorado.

03

Student research

Paid undergraduate research and student instructor roles help FLC students build real experience in emerging technologies.
